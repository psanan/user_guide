* Writing things down  
* Understanding

* Design docs

* Deleting things

* In-person collaboration  
* In-person socialization and fun  
* Working with ethical, thoughtful, well-rounded, and intrinsically motivated people  
* Quality

#### Communication preferences

I like to talk in person.

I don't always have the energy to make the first move.

Unless I have headphones on, go ahead and interrupt me at my desk.

I speak quickly and even native English speakers find me hard to understand, so please don't be shy about asking me to repeat.

I also very much like to express myself in writing.

I (like most people, probably) don't like it that important information often ends up in hard-to-find chat messages. I love it when I can find information again because it's in a doc or bug.

#### Name and Cultural Background
